Who can see it

  • Coaches and the owner see member profiles, health answers, emergency contacts, bookings and Open Gym details, so they can run classes safely.
  • Other members may see your first name on class lists, and your first name, last initial and scores on leaderboards. They never see your contact or health details.
  • Service providers who process data for us under contract: Supabase (database and sign-in), Stripe (payments), GitHub Pages (hosts the app's files, no member data), our app developer (support and maintenance), and, if the gym uses one, the door-lock provider (receives only an "unlock" signal and your name).

We don't sell your data or use it for advertising.

Where it's stored

Member data is held by Supabase in its UK or EU region. Stripe may process payment data in the US under the UK's approved safeguards for international transfers.

Your rights

You can:

  • Get a copy of your data: Me > Account > Download.
  • Correct it: Me > Edit.
  • Delete your account: Me > Account > Delete. This removes your personal data except payment records we must keep.
  • Withdraw consent for marketing, photos or health information, object to how we use your data, or ask us to restrict it: email us.

We'll respond within one month. If you're unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

Cookies and device storage

The app doesn't use tracking or advertising cookies. It stores your sign-in session and a few preferences on your device so you stay signed in. Stripe's checkout pages set their own cookies to prevent fraud.

Children

Members must be 16 or over. Under-18s need a parent or guardian's agreement.

Changes

If we change this notice we'll update the date above and tell members in the app about significant changes.
