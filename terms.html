These terms are between you and [Gym legal entity name], trading as CrossFit House of Wolves ("we", "the gym"). By joining, booking a class or using Open Gym you agree to them, along with the participation waiver and our privacy notice.

1. Joining

  • You must be 16 or over. Under-18s need a parent or guardian to agree to these terms.
  • You must complete the health questionnaire honestly and tell a coach about any change in your health or any injury before training.
  • If you answer "yes" to a health question, a coach will talk to you before your first session and may ask you to check with your doctor.

2. Memberships and payments

  • Monthly memberships are paid in advance by card or Direct Debit through Stripe, on the same date each month.
  • The Introductory Month is for new members only. It's discounted for the first month, then continues as CFHOW Unlimited at the full price unless you switch plans before it renews.
  • Class packs and drop-ins are paid up front and must be used before they expire (10-class Hyrox pass: 2 months; drop-in: 30 days). Unused classes aren't refunded.
  • Weekly plans (for example 3 a Week) run Monday to Sunday. Unused classes don't carry over.
  • If a payment fails, booking is paused until it's paid. Update your payment details in Me > Manage billing.
  • We'll give you at least 30 days' notice of any price change.

3. Changing, freezing and cancelling

  • Change plan any time in the app. Moving between monthly plans is adjusted (prorated) on your next payment.
  • Freeze for 7 to 92 days (holidays, injury, illness). Payments pause and your bookings in that period are released.
  • Cancel in Me > Manage billing. Your membership runs to the end of the period you've paid for. [Gym to confirm: any minimum term or notice period.]
  • You can also cancel within 14 days of first joining for a refund, less the value of any classes you've used.

4. Booking classes

  • Classes open for booking 7 days ahead.
  • Cancel more than 2 hours before a class for free. Later cancellations and no-shows count as a used class (and use a credit on packs).
  • If a class is full, join the waitlist. If a space opens, you're booked in automatically and notified, so check your notifications.
  • We may cancel or change a class (for example if a coach is ill). If we do, we'll notify you and return any credit used.

5. Open Gym

  • Open Gym is included with CFHOW Unlimited and the Introductory Month.
  • Book a slot in the app, reserve the kit you need and confirm the safety checks.
  • Only use equipment you've been coached on. Don't lift heavy alone: use safety bars or train with someone.
  • Clean and re-rack everything, and check out when you leave.

6. Door access

  • Buzz yourself in from the app when you're at the gym for a booked class or Open Gym slot. The app checks your location when you tap.
  • Don't buzz in anyone else or hold the door for people who haven't booked. Every buzz is logged for security.
  • Misusing door access may lead to your membership being suspended.

7. Behaviour and safety

  • Follow coaches' instructions and gym rules, and respect other members and the equipment.
  • We may suspend or end a membership for unsafe or abusive behaviour, or misuse of door access. We'll refund any unused prepaid amount.

8. Participation waiver

CrossFit, Hyrox and Open Gym training involve strenuous exercise and a risk of injury. You take part at your own risk and are responsible for training within your ability and telling us about health issues. Nothing in these terms limits our liability for death or personal injury caused by our negligence, or anything else that can't be limited by law. Look after your belongings: we're not responsible for loss or damage unless it's caused by our negligence.

9. Photos

We only use photos of you on social media if you've said yes. Change your choice any time by emailing us.

10. Changes to these terms

We may update these terms. We'll tell you in the app at least 30 days before a change that affects you, and you can cancel before it takes effect.

11. Law

These terms are governed by the law of Scotland, and the Scottish courts have jurisdiction.
